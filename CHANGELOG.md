# Changelog

Notable changes to SimCruise, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Dates are release dates, not the survey dates of the data a release uses. The release notes written for Sunday Driver 1.0.0 to 1.0.8 are preserved word for word in [docs/history.md](docs/history.md).

## SimCruise 1.1.0 (unreleased)

Renamed from Sunday Driver (commit `2f1d1cf`). The work below follows the 8 November 2026 design-panel report kept as [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md) and runs as parallel streams; the owner finalises this list at release.

### In progress

- **Controls** — speed-aware cap on keyboard and pad steering (the rack still allowed 18.8° at 45 mph where about 3° is usable), slower pedal and steering ramps, a keyboard-tap bench, and one-press recovery that notices a roof-down or stuck car, prompts for R and searches every road, not only main roads.
- **Mountain roads** — streamed roads are now built as road benches: a smooth profile measured along the curving road, a flat shelf with superelevation in rural curves, and cut and fill slopes that meet the hillside, instead of a terrain grid that skipped steep side slopes and capped corrections at 1.5 m. Streamed tiles use a 128 grid drawn adaptively, so only cells on the bench gain triangles. On the first 7.7 km of the Tail of the Dragon (US 129, Deals Gap) the RMS slope change under the tyres fell from 1.19° to 0.20° and the worst from 21.6°/m to 3.3°/m; the scripted test drive (`checks/RoadDrive.gd`, new) rolled the car within 0.7 km before and now finishes both directions without leaving the ground. `checks/RoadProfile.gd` gains `--dir=` and `--classes=` to measure any streamed tiles.
- **Bridges** — mapped bridges are built as decks with asphalt, edge and centre lines, concrete parapets that keep the car on them, a slab and piers, instead of the car driving down into the creek, valley or rail yard below. The helper sets each deck straight between the road benches of its approaches (with a short end fit for approach embankments), follows a bridge across z14 tile edges, remembers its end heights in the cache so every tile it crosses agrees, and keeps the ground under it bare earth. On the Cheyenne I-180 viaduct the test drive had wheel lifts and 1.5 g jolts before and now crosses in both directions with no airtime and at most 0.55 g. `checks/RoadDrive.gd` also reports impacts and its driver lifts off in a slide.
- **Roads** — a smoother driving surface: the 32 × 32 tile height grid produced a slope step at every grid crease in both the visible mesh and the collider, which launched the car; the fix takes the roadbed off the raw grid.
- **Light** — one sun instead of two (the sky panorama's unclipped sun added unshadowed light on top of the `DirectionalLight3D`, giving a shadow-to-sun ratio near 0.5 where photographs show 0.18 to 0.32), longer shadow range, tonemap white point, debanding and anisotropy settings, and the real clock for the sun angle instead of a fixed June afternoon.
- **Immersion** — HUD levels (Off / Glance / Full) with translucent panels instead of opaque bars that were on by default, sound on by default with tyre, wind and gravel layers added to the engine, and a driver head that lags and settles instead of a camera welded to the chassis.
- **Vegetation** — canopies with backlight, occlusion and warm tint instead of opaque dark blobs, near fade and alpha-to-coverage, mown verges and textured swaying field clumps instead of knee-high grass spikes.
- **Open-source project setup** — the repository moved to the `simcruisegame` organization and is public. Added `SECURITY.md` (private vulnerability reporting), `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` (Contributor Covenant 2.1), issue and pull-request templates, `.editorconfig`, and `scripts/check-helper.sh`, which runs the map helper's 18 offline tests and checks that it bundles. GitHub secret scanning with push protection and Dependabot security alerts are on. The version now lives in `config/version` in `game/project.godot`; the bundle identifier is `io.github.simcruisegame.simcruise`. The README is shorter and the detailed sections moved to `docs/` (install, controls, technical overview, data sources, building, studies, related projects).
- **Docs and release** — newcomer README; `docs/roadmap.md`, `docs/history.md` and `docs/architecture-notes.md`; this changelog; `CONTRIBUTING.md`; `scripts/make-dmg.sh` and the `dmg` mode of `scripts/build-macos.sh` (disk image with an Applications shortcut and a first-launch Gatekeeper note); `.gitattributes`; `.gitignore` additions; former-name notes in the `lab/` studies; the helper's User-Agent now identifies as SimCruise; the helper's modules, JSON datasets and `vendor/` moved from the repository root into `helper/`, so `scripts/build-macos.sh` compiles `helper/atlas.mjs` and the root holds only the top-level folders and project files.

### Calibration guard

Every stream keeps the headless `game/checks/Track.gd` run (`--fixed-fps 60`) inside its bands: 0–60 mph 7.97 ± 0.10 s, 70–0 mph 173 ± 3 ft, 300 ft skidpad 0.89 ± 0.01 g. Baseline on `2f1d1cf`: 7.948 s, 173.0 ft, 0.885 g. `RideCheck.gd`, `TurningCircle.gd` (30.35 ft left, 30.58 ft right at 1.0 and 1.8 m/s against the 30.2 ft specification) and `Wall.gd` (70 mph into a 15 cm wall, not crossed) also pass on that commit.

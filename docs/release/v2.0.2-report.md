## Bugs

Fixed in 2.0.2 ([bugs.md](bugs.md)): P1 (a traffic contact flung the car), P2 (the 10 cm road step at a tile border on CA-1), the Mustang tumbling and exploding off the road (611f955), the Tune car steering ratio reading 0.0, the Shipping build, the old weights left by an upgrade from 2.0.0.

Known issues: the dashboard gauges (the speedometer needle and the other dials do not follow the car); P3 (strong motion blur at 25-30 mph); P4 (Tail of the Dragon road gap); P5 (an occasional road gap at a tile border on CA-1); P6 (on the Tail of the Dragon the trees can drop out for a frame and the cabin exposure flickers; older than 2.0.2); P7 (the Miata has no ABS: about 60 % brake trigger locks the fronts and the car cannot turn); P8 (a light throttle at low engine speed is weak, as in 2.0.1: the throttle-plate model is off). From 2.0.1: the Corvette C8's 0-60, the Mustang's brake and launch spins, the Miata's dash clock.

## Not done

- A 20-minute Shipping soak and the frame-rate run (2.0.1 measured them; not repeated for this patch).
- The installer test was run silently by script, not by hand through the setup's pages; the game was started from the installed folder, not by a click on the Start menu entry.

# SimCruise 2.0.2: what you should notice

This page is for the player. It tells you what to try in 2.0.2 and what must feel different from 2.0.1. The language is simple English (ASD-STE100 style). The full list of changes is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Before you start

1. Install SimCruise 2.0.2 with the setup file. The setup replaces 2.0.1. Your settings stay.
2. Start SimCruise from the Start menu.
3. Use the Xbox controller or the keyboard. The controls are in [docs/controls.md](../controls.md).

## 1. The pause menu

1. Push Menu on the controller (Tab on the keyboard).
2. Push LB or RB (Q or E) to change the page.

You see four pages:

- **Drive**: continue, Where to?, plan a route, famous roads, traffic and the road atlas.
- **Your car**: the car, the gearbox, Tune car and the headlights.
- **Seat & view**: the lens, head motion, screen size and eye distance.
- **Settings**: sound, HUD, rumble, and the stick and trigger settings.

What to look for: every row looks like a green US road sign. The text is large. You can read it from the sofa.

## 2. Your seat and your car

1. Stop the car. Open the pause menu. Go to **Your car**.
2. Select **Seat & view**. Set the seat height, the seat fore and aft, the view tilt and the view angle.
3. Look at the road behind the panel. The game runs. The car stays on its brakes.
4. Select **Tune car**. Look at the steering ratio. It must show the real ratio of the car (15:1 on the Miata), not 0.0.

What to look for: the game keeps your seat for each car. When you change the setup while you drive, it goes on at the next stop.

## 3. Views and looking around

1. Push Y on the controller (C on the keyboard). The view changes: seat, hood, near chase, far chase, scenic.
2. In the seat or hood view, move the right stick. Your head turns up to 110 degrees each way.
3. Release the right stick. The view goes back to the road.

What to look for: at high speed the view becomes a little wider.

## 4. Traffic

1. Open the pause menu. Go to **Drive**.
2. Set **Traffic** to Normal, Light or Off.

What to look for: when you select Off, the traffic cars go away at once. The game keeps your choice for the next drive.

## 5. The GPS

1. Open the pause menu. Go to **Drive**.
2. Select **Famous roads**. Select one: Ocean Drive, the Tail of the Dragon, Big Sur or Monument Valley.
3. Drive. Follow the route.

Or plan your own route:

1. Select **Plan a route**. A map with a cross opens.
2. Move the cross with the left stick. Push A to add a stop. Push X to remove a stop.
3. Use LT and RT to zoom. Push Y to start the route.

Or use **Where to?**: find a place, then make it your stop.

What to look for:

- A green sign at the top of the screen shows the next turn, its distance and the street name.
- Magenta chevrons on the road show your lane, 5 to 60 m ahead.
- A magenta arrow board stands at the corner of the next turn.
- A round map at the bottom right turns with the car. It shows the route, the stops, the distance and the arrival time.
- If you leave the route for more than 3 seconds, the GPS finds a new route from where you are.

## 6. The new speed display

What to look for: the speed is large at the bottom left. The gear is in amber. A rev bar shows the red zone. Push X (F2) to change the HUD between Off, Glance and Full.

## 7. How the cars drive

- **Miata, in a turn**: the car takes the load in a turn more quickly (roll centres). When you lift off in a turn, the Torsen differential keeps the rear more stable.
- **1965 Mustang off the road**: drive the Mustang off the road on the Tail of the Dragon or on Big Sur. The car must not jump, fly or show a speed of hundreds of mph. Before, it did.
- **Contact with traffic**: if your car touches a traffic car, it must not fly away. Before, a touch could throw the car at hundreds of mph.
- **The throttle**: the trigger feels as in 2.0.1. A new throttle model is in the game but off, because it made the cars spin when you start in a turn.

## 8. The world

- **Grass**: stop next to a lawn or a verge. You can see thin blades near the car. The road and the paths stay clear.
- **Aerial photographs**: on Ocean Drive, look at the ground far away. The photographs have no dark sun shadows that do not agree with the game's sun.
- **Big Sur (CA-1)**: drive south of Bixby Bridge. The step of about 10 cm at a tile border is gone. The car must not jolt there.
- **Thin things**: window frames, rails and poles flicker less.

## Known issues in 2.0.2

These problems are known. They are not fixed in 2.0.2.

- **The dashboard gauges do not work.** The speedometer needle and the other dials do not follow the car. Read the speed on the HUD.
- **Small gaps in the road.** On the Tail of the Dragon, in the hairpins about 515 m from the start, there is a strip of about 1.3 m with no road surface. On Big Sur (CA-1) there is a similar strip at one tile border. The car can jolt a little there.
- **Trees can drop out for a moment.** On the Tail of the Dragon the trees beside the road can go away for one frame. The light in the cabin can flicker. This problem is older than 2.0.2.
- **Strong motion blur.** At 25 to 30 mph, near objects at the side of the road are blurred too much.
- **The Miata has no ABS.** A hard press on the brake trigger (about 60 %) locks the front wheels. Then the car cannot turn. Press the trigger less to steer while you brake.
- **Light throttle is weak at low engine speed**, as in 2.0.1. Press the trigger more to pull away.
- **From 2.0.1:** the Corvette is slower from 0 to 60 mph than the real car (3.2 s, not 2.8 s). The Mustang can spin when you brake very hard or start in a turn with full throttle. The Miata's dash clock shows the start time, not the time of day.

## Tell us

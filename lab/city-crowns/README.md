# Surveyed crowns and occupied tree wells

Cambridge GIS and the University of Vermont Spatial Analysis Laboratory published individual 2024 LiDAR watershed polygons and the 98th percentile height above local ground. The municipal inventory provides surveyed trunks, diameter, species and planting dates where recorded. The normal canopy helper requests polygons only around reciprocal inventory–canopy matches. In three Cambridge neighborhoods, 62 accepted 16-direction outlines had mean plan-view intersection-over-union of 0.893 against a fresh, finer query of the city polygons, compared with 0.526 for the preceding nominal circular outlines. The mean error at sixteen interleaved, withheld directions fell from 1.63 m to 0.34 m. These numbers measure agreement with the published geometry. The survey does not describe each branch. [City crown methods and PDDL license](https://www.cambridgema.gov/gis/gisdatadictionary/environmental/environmental_treecanopy2024) · [City tree inventory](https://www.cambridgema.gov/gis/gisdatadictionary/environmental/environmental_streettrees)

One crown must contain exactly one eligible known trunk. Tile boundaries, holes, multipart or ambiguous shapes, inconsistent heights and incompatible planting dates abstain. The display checks the observation year against the simulated reference year or the current year, with a three-year tolerance. A single low-priority worker transforms the existing tree meshes. At most eight models are visible and twelve cached. Earlier eligible optical peaks keep their original fallback and collision geometry. [Source fidelity receipt](source-fidelity.json)

## Columbia Street

The independently mapped public sidewalk wells 7068 and 7107 contain municipal honeylocusts 281 and 280. Both wells explicitly report a tree. The 2018 city photogrammetry places the trunk centers 0.76 m and 0.86 m beyond the pavement edge. At each trunk the code measures the full paved cross-section three times, at the trunk and 3 m in either direction. The central sections are 8.89 m and 8.75 m wide. The game's generic road envelope was 11 m wide. A model-side check protects the center of the simulated travel lanes, rejects buildings and verifies road orientation. Restoring a visual trunk leaves the preceding collision bodies in place. [Road survey](https://www.cambridgema.gov/gis/gisdatadictionary/basemap/basemap_roads) · [Occupied tree wells](https://www.cambridgema.gov/gis/gisdatadictionary/basemap/basemap_sidewalks)

The two November 12, 2022 photographs are 14 m apart. The raw GPS track falls about 2.25 m left of the measured roadway center. We used 106 foreground samples per double-yellow line in the first photograph, an assumed 1.65 m camera height and 81° horizontal field of view to estimate camera position and direction. Both cameras use the same 3.78 m lateral correction, zero longitudinal correction, heading 25.71° and camera pitched up 2.97°. The camera archive supplies no calibrated intrinsics. The second photograph and the trees were held out of pose fitting. The unadjusted GPS comparison also recovered the same two surveyed trunks. All active trunk collider positions, radii and heights matched before, after and on repeat. Both frames maintained four tire contacts. Switching the same camera to 2017 returned pixel-identical frames in all three states. The game retains opaque summer foliage on the nearly leafless November trees. ## Source transfer and reproduction

A cold run on z16 tile 19825/24237 made six requests including the optical canopy and existing civic inventory. The three additional records used 51,349 bytes for the crown intersections, 4,407 bytes for occupied wells and 304,222 bytes for the road geometry shared across Cambridge. The warm run made zero requests. The helper returned 17 crown profiles and six corroborated curbside wells in that tile. Broken or unavailable sources leave normal geometry in place. The extra lookups are limited to this registered publisher's extent and compatible trunks. Cambridge uses PDDL 1.0. The GeoServices polygon decoder reads Esri's published 2D FeatureCollection protocol with the existing pbf library. [PDDL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1.0/) · [Esri protocol](https://github.com/Esri/arcgis-pbf/blob/main/proto/FeatureCollection/FeatureCollection.proto)

Run the checks from the project root. The optional visual check stages the current game in a supplied scratch directory, starts small serial native windows and writes PNGs and collider receipts there. It requires an imported project, Godot 4.7 and Bun. The comparison photographs by morganwahl are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, © Grab and KartaView contributors. The gallery crops and resizes them alongside the game. [Photograph license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

## Other candidate sources

Ma and colleagues released 6,005,690 individual New York City crown polygons and tree attributes under CC BY 4.0. The laser surveys date to August 2013 and March–April 2014. We inspected the directory of the 205 MB archive with two range reads totaling 196,608 bytes. Manhattan and the other boroughs have separate shapefiles. This is a documented historical offline source. Live placement would require a dated stem inventory, a spatially queryable index and independent registration checks. Seattle's 2021 product describes individual trees as fitted circles, which cannot constrain asymmetry. [NYC data and license](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.20522895.v2) · [Original methods and dates](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9941473/)

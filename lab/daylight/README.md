# Road-camera daylight and observed weather

In default play the four documented camera locations below use the sun at the photographed hour and a locally archived sky estimate. The effect is full strength within 145 metres of the published coordinates and fades out at 350 metres. The map shows **Photo daylight** and the date while the archived estimate dominates. A nearby independently dated street camera takes precedence when its time differs by more than one hour. A time alone supplies a sun direction without pretending to know cloud cover at that hour.

**Find a place → Weather** enables the existing NOAA METAR lookup and replaces the archived weather. Within 40 km of a station reporting CLR or SKC, at least 10 km valid visibility and no weather phenomena, the visible field uses the clear-sky HDR. Automated stations may miss high clouds. An unavailable, obscured, distant or ambiguous report retains the established weather rendering. Existing precipitation-related cloud, fog and directional lighting stay active. Ordinary play keeps daylight even when a current report comes from overnight.

All scores compare fixed image regions after transforming embedded camera color profiles to sRGB. A photograph’s local exposure, cloud positions and color processing remain uncertain. La Sal’s left/right foreground patches remain 64/79 luma levels too bright. The measured clear sky across all four cameras, at their actual hours, also tested well, but using it at the generic afternoon made two cameras worse. The prototype three-wavelength single-scattering field was too dark at Wyoming’s recorded hour, so only the verified high-cloud case uses it.

## Data, license and runtime

No new network service runs in the game. The three authored sky fields total about 91 KB. The existing, user-enabled weather worker sends a rounded area to NOAA’s public Aviation Weather Center and caches its recent station reports for 15 minutes. Four historical summaries are embedded as offline evidence. The prototype queried four daily ERA5 samples in a small personal research run. Open-Meteo makes the returned data redistributable under CC BY 4.0 with attribution. Its hosted free API has a separate noncommercial-use condition; the game never queries it. Values were normalized into a local optical class, fade and, at La Sal, an estimated veil. [NOAA Aviation Weather API](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/) · [historical ERA5 API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api) · [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [IEM station archive](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/download.phtml)

Both lighting photographs are by Greg Zaal, CC0. The clear HDR was exposure-normalized by its measured solar disk, cropped above the horizon and encoded as square-root radiance. The partly cloudy HDR supplies the preserved highlights, original image-based lighting and an area-weighted cloud-rank mask. The archival high-cloud case uses a 17-sun-elevation offline Rayleigh/Mie lookup plus a veil estimated from direct and diffuse irradiance. It cannot reproduce individual clouds. [Kloofendal 43° clear (pure sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_43d_clear_puresky) · [Kloofendal 48° partly cloudy](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy) · [Bruneton’s scattering equations](https://ebruneton.github.io/precomputed_atmospheric_scattering/atmosphere/functions.glsl.html)

## Reproduce

- From the source root, run `node lab/daylight/conditions-check.mjs` and `godot --headless --path game --script res://checks/DaylightWeatherCheck.gd`. The checks use no external API.
- To author the textures again, pass the shipped `game/art/day.hdr`, Poly Haven’s checksum-verified 1k `kloofendal_43d_clear_puresky_1k.hdr`, and a destination directory to `python lab/daylight/bake.py`. The authoring computer requires Python, NumPy and Pillow. All three rebaked files matched the checked-in bytes.
- Use the normal game’s `--reference LAT LON HEADING HFOV OUT.jpg PITCH ROLL --reference-epoch EPOCH` with the values in validation.json. Include `--landscape-reference` for the three western sites and `--shrub-reference --soil-reference` for the two Utah views. The archive code is the same one used by a player on the road. Start from a separately staged build and cache for native QA.
- Five isolated Godot 4.7.2 Mobile Metal sessions ran consecutively on an Apple A18 Pro. The stationary 960 × 720 lighting benchmark recorded median wall-frame intervals of 8.1–8.7 ms across controls and alternatives. Differences within 1 ms were smaller than the noise. This driver always returned zero in the GPU-only timer. At Chula the actual KFCI report at 06:56 UTC on November 8 2026 was replayed through the Weather button and ordinary station read. The unknown, fog and overcast checks used injected inputs. Turning Weather off restored the original local surveyed sky.

An additional ordinary US 163 drive supplied neither a shutter-time argument nor a custom daylight override. The game selected the archived 2023 epoch by geographic position, moved 14.40 metres on player throttle and reported four wheel contacts at the end.

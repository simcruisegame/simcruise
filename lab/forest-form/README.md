# Forest form from mapped stands

The live helper samples a 30 m vegetation-type grid after the driving tile receives its canopy peaks. Each zoom-14 observation covers sixteen driving tiles. The 2025 LANDFIRE Existing Vegetation Type legend identifies 37 tree systems with conifer physiognomy and spruce, fir, hemlock or lodgepole in the system name. Dwarf, treeline, krummholz and mixed-evergreen systems are excluded. NLCD evergreen class 42 must agree in the same cell and in at least two of its four cardinal neighbors. The 2021 CONUS and 2016 Alaska NLCD grids are independently georeferenced. GeoServer can return a larger footprint than requested when it reprojects the grid. Both vegetation products draw on remote sensing and can share errors. LANDFIRE recommends applying its classifications over areas larger than their native cells. Only generic Meta/WRI canopy peaks qualify. The radius, height, rotation, ground anchor and trunk collider stay on the original row. One in eight eligible peaks, selected by a stable coordinate hash, keeps its earlier form. Surveyed municipal trees, including records that fall back to a canopy anchor, and the bundled Virginia field captures take precedence. Mixed, broadleaf, sagebrush, paved and ambiguous cells keep their earlier forms. The model includes a tapered trunk, folded three-dimensional needle fans and a shaded interior at three levels of detail. The new meshes have 4,482, 2,302 and 780 triangles. ## Streaming and provenance

A background worker reads one region at a time after live canopy arrives. It requests at most two 96 KiB responses, with a 5.5 s timeout per response and a one-second minimum interval per provider. A district with no candidate LANDFIRE systems skips NLCD. Both raw grids are retained for up to one year. The derived observation and ordinary absence use a 180-day cache. A temporary failure uses a 30-minute cooldown and preserves an older successful observation. The existing source-cache size budget covers these files. The scene reads the packed bits, both product years and the pinned legend hash. Hawaiʻi and the territories currently have no validated pair and use their existing trees. Four small offline masks cover the Wyoming camera, an independently accepted Maine district and a coastal Alaska district.

The read-only helper command is `atlas forest-form CACHE X Y`, using zoom-14 Web Mercator integer coordinates. Each returned X/Y cell is a stand-level inference. Whole-source dataset downloads and manual place names are unnecessary.

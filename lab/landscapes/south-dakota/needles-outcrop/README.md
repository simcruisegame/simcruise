# Granite on Needles Highway, South Dakota

In **Find a place**, enter **43.84018, -103.53945**. Start west on Needles Highway, approximately 270°. The outcrop enters the left of the stock cockpit about 28 m along this approach.

Three photographs by w_lemay from two handheld camera stations provide parallax. July 28, 2023 frame 149021335 registers the camera and supplies a small granite detail sample. Frame 149021337 was held out from the granite reconstruction, material sampling and shape fit. Six distant matches constrain its camera orientation. Its position was interpolated by capture time between two adjacent registered cameras, about one second away on either side. Local timestamps were interpreted as Mountain Daylight Time.

The mesh uses independent camera masks, dense stereo, measured rock-front depths and elevations from USGS flight lines. It closes the solid behind the observed walls. Two geographically fixed rock textures reveal only stone in the source photographs. Where a tree hides a face in one photograph, a valid second view can supply color. Mineral grain covers the remaining unseen faces. Tree crowns and the distant forest still differ visibly.

The local 0.25 m bare-earth grid cuts through parts of the observed wall. Lower optical envelopes and cells in measured clear lines of sight constrain the rendered ground there. The terrain mesh and collider share triangles. Road footprints, tile edges and changed-source fingerprints retain the ordinary road surface. The game reads about 1 MB from its bundle and makes no requests for this outcrop. Other places continue using the normal nationwide terrain.

The comparison uses the actual stock driver, the measured local terrain and matched Godot Metal renders. All reconstruction and assessment units, input URLs, licenses, EXIF and camera assumptions are in the receipt. The full-resolution raw photographs remain available under CC BY-SA 2.0 from their individual Wikimedia Commons records.

The authoring directory contains a reproducible final mesh bake and its geographically registered observations. The measurements are sufficient to rebuild the local solid without downloading the statewide 426 GB laser archive. They preserve the distinction between the source photographs, intermediate survey measurements and the final scene.

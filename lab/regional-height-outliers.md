# Isolated holes in the distant elevation raster

Some zoom-10 Terrarium tiles have clusters of false elevations. In the Virginia 90 km scene, two native pixels encoded −667 m in the middle of a largely flat area. Bilinear interpolation produced a −602.17 m vertex at longitude −77.8066151, latitude 37.6107227. The zoom-12 tile returned +41.08 m at that position. The separately published USGS 1/3 arc-second bare-earth raster gave +42.35 m, with a 7 by 7 neighborhood ranging from +41.08 to +44.11 m. The erroneous coarse values were present in the original downloaded RGB bytes. The source production stage responsible is unknown. [USGS 3DEP Virginia tile](https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Elevation/13/TIFF/current/n38w078/USGS_13_n38w078.tif) and [original Terrarium source](https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/terrarium/10/290/396.png).

The optional repair runs before skyline selection and triangulation. It looks for deep pits isolated in both horizontal axes, checks a 16-direction collar and a wider low-relief neighborhood, and verifies at two zoom levels higher. Only when the more detailed raster is locally smooth and agrees with the surrounding collar may its median replace the affected coarse pixel and its limited resampling halo. The raw files remain available in the source cache. Boundary candidates without a trustworthy collar, rugged ground, coastal mixtures, absent or malformed witnesses, and pits corroborated by both resolutions remain untouched. The high-resolution witness comes from the same provider. The USGS comparisons are external validation rather than runtime requests. The module makes up to two witness reads taken from the existing four refinement slots. Each is limited to 300 kB and at most 1.6 seconds within the caller’s remaining deadline.

The latter three residuals need different evidence. The Alaska point is near a sharp transition in the finer source. The Hawai‘i official raster is zero for all 49 pixels in its local window. Real below-sea-level land in Death Valley precludes clamping all negative elevations. A region-wide offline replay required 3–24 ms for the screening pass on this Mac, with no source reads in Utah, Hawaii, Alaska or Death Valley. The Virginia witness was 114,963 compressed bytes, Nebraska 108,628 bytes.

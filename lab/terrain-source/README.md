# Ground beneath the tires

The same source and sampler run anywhere a USGS seamless 1/3-arc-second product is indexed. The packaged directory lists 1,449 geographic products and occupies 14 KB. At runtime geotiff.js reads the TIFF directory and the compressed blocks needed for the road-grading window. A product ETag pins every range to the same edition. The cache, request spacing, backoff and HTTP range validation are shared across helper processes. At ordinary test sites a cold neighborhood needed two or three elevation reads; at a four-degree intersection it took eight. A repeat needed zero. Roads, photos and other layers have separate budgets. Publication dates and survey capture dates differ.

The raster spacing is approximately 10 m north/south. It supplies ground elevation. It cannot establish centimeter-scale road crown or a bridge deck height. Mapped elevated roads retain their previous surface, established authored districts retain their survey, and overlapping game tiles use the same global coordinates. Where a source is missing the prior terrain remains available. The next incoming tile receives boundary heights from its established neighbors. An occupied tile keeps its collider.

## Reproduce the checks

From the source root, run `bun lab/terrain-source/check-windows.mjs /tmp/sd-3dep-cache` for real range access, cached repeats, a four-degree join, Alaska, New Hampshire, Puerto Rico, Guam and an unsupported ocean control. The observed cold and warm counts are in `window-check.json`. Use `godot --headless --path game --script "$PWD/lab/check-terrain-edges.gd"` for the mixed-product joining check. `python3 lab/terrain-source/replay.py` needs NumPy and replays the per-site vertical comparisons offline.

Each row of the compressed reference array represents an occupied 5 m cell on a mapped paved road. The estimator takes the median ground-class USGS laser return in that cell, removes mapped grade-separated structures and their immediate approaches, samples both meshes at its horizontal position, and removes one median vertical offset per district and model. This tests local relief. Some raster products may be derived from the same flight, so the results establish agreement with held-out observations without establishing independent acquisition. `validation.json` includes the five sites with usable held-out ground samples that remain in the project; a sixth, in a withdrawn survey corridor, was removed. Honolulu's 90th-percentile error fell from 0.257 to 0.166 m after distinguishing surface road bridges from underground corridors. Seattle's figure fell from 2.491 to 0.163 m.

On a 47 m K Street course in Anchorage the prior Miata reached 0.46 m/s and rolled back. With the new surface it drove through at up to 4.62 m/s and retained four tire contacts for 708/708 physics ticks on the original fixed-input course. The ordinary ground query and rendered triangles then agreed with vertical tire raycasts within 0.013 mm. A separate accepted laser crossfall can refine this surface. The photograph in the gallery shows nearby I Street, looking north. The K Street driving result and I Street observer are distinct.
